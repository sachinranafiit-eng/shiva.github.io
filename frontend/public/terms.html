Enquiry versus booking

A WhatsApp message, call or website enquiry is a request for response. It does not by itself reserve a technician, confirm a service slot or create a charge.

Scope and price approval

Displayed prices are indicative unless specifically confirmed in writing for your job. Visit charges, labour, parts/materials and any legally applicable taxes are confirmed before work starts. Extra work or materials require customer approval.

Cancellation, refund and service concerns

Cancellation, rescheduling, refund or remedy terms should be confirmed for the relevant paid booking before payment. Nothing on this page limits rights available under applicable consumer law.

Safety and access

Electrical and technical work must be carried out with appropriate competency, authorization and safety measures applicable to the work. Site access, isolation/shutdown and special permissions should be agreed before starting.

Complaints and support

For a service concern, cancellation request or invoice question, call +91 9259599151. Provide the service date/booking reference when available. This contact is not represented as a statutory grievance-officer designation unless the business formally appoints and publishes one.
